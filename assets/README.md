Total unique geometry is approximately 1,260 triangles before reuse. The
source contains preview-only camera and material objects, which are not part
of any FBX export. Every FBX contains only its named mesh object.

All 12 FBX files above were imported through Roblox Studio's 3D Importer on
2026-09-29. The import queue completed 12/12, and the resulting MeshPart IDs
and imported dimensions were copied into `default.project.json` under
`ServerStorage.MapMeshKit`. No asset in this kit remains pending import. The
FBX exporter uses `global_scale=0.01` so Studio imports them at the dimensions
listed above (with Y/Z exchanged for Roblox coordinates). Keep each template's
`InitialSize` and `Size` equal to its imported dimensions; a MeshId-only Rojo
template renders clones at the wrong scale even when their `Size` is set later.

The FBX files themselves are source exports, not the Roblox assets. Do not
substitute made-up MeshIds or third-party assets. Decorative mesh instances
are non-collidable; gameplay collision uses simple native parts where needed.

## Curse mesh kit (vertical slice)

Six additional original, single-mesh low-poly assets have editable source in
`source/blender/steal_a_curse_curses.blend`; the reproducible authoring script
is `source/blender/generate_curses.py`. Run
`source/blender/validate_curses.py` with Blender to verify FBX reimports,
dimensions, and triangle counts. The twelve environment FBX files above are
unchanged. These six FBX files were imported through Studio's 3D Importer on
2026-09-30; the resulting IDs and imported MeshPart sizes were read from the

## Final haunted-world environment extension

The [final modular environment kit](FINAL_ENVIRONMENT.md) adds twelve original
single-material meshes for the manor, collection courts, cemetery, and world
silhouettes. Its separate editable source is
`source/blender/steal_a_curse_final_environment.blend`, generated by
`source/blender/generate_final_environment.py`; FBXs use the `final_` prefix under
`export/meshes/`. The previous environment and Curse source files and exports
are retained.

The extension totals **2,468 unique triangles across 12 meshes**. Together with
the original environment kit, the project has 24 unique environment meshes and
about 3,728 source triangles before reuse. Including the six Curse meshes,
the three source kits total 30 meshes and about 5,808 unique triangles. These
source totals do not describe the number of repeated scene instances or runtime
draw cost.

All twelve new FBXs were imported with Studio's 3D Importer. Their actual
MeshPart `MeshId`, `InitialSize`, and `Size` are recorded in
[the verified manifest](source/blender/final_environment_manifest.json) and
mapped under `ServerStorage.MapMeshKit` in `default.project.json`. See
[FINAL_ENVIRONMENT.md](FINAL_ENVIRONMENT.md) for the module-by-module triangle
counts, bounds, verified IDs, centered placement rules, reproduction commands,
validation checks, and actual-geometry preview. The Roblox place was not
published by the mesh import workflow.
